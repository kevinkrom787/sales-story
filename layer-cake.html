The Layer Cake

Technical deals are won layer by layer — engineer, manager, director, VP, C-suite — each a different altitude with a different appetite. The win is one coherent story, retold in each layer's language and laddered up to the organization's priorities. Pick a priority, weave the thread — then hover a layer for the real reason they'll champion it.

The trick

Five altitudes, one cake. The engineer won't sign the contract and the CTO won't run the install — but every layer has to taste a version of the same story, told in their language and tied to the metric they own at their altitude.

The thread is what holds the cake together: the engineer's reclaimed hours roll up into the manager's throughput, into the director's roadmap, into the VP's budget, into the line on the C-suite's board deck. Break the thread at any layer and the deal slides apart there.

And here's the part nobody says out loud: under every business case is a personal one — looking smart, getting promoted, climbing the ladder. The champion who bets on you is really betting on their own next title. Align the win with what they secretly want and they'll sell up for you. Hover any layer for the real reason.
